Problem 87: Prime Power Triples
The smallest number expressible as the sum of a prime square, prime cube, and prime fourth power is 28. There are exactly four numbers below fifty that can be expressed in such a way: 28 = 2²+2³+2⁴, 33 = 3²+2³+2⁴, 49 = 5²+2³+2⁴, 47 = 2²+3³+2⁴. How many numbers below fifty million can be expressed as the sum of a prime square, prime cube, and prime fourth power?
