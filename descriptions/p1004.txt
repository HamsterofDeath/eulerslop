<p>A positive integer is called <i>balanced</i> if the size of its longest strictly decreasing subsequence (in its decimal expression) is the same as the size of its longest non-strictly increasing subsequence.</p>

<p>For example, the number $77429$ is balanced, because both its longest strictly decreasing subsequence ($742$) and its longest non-strictly increasing subsequence ($779$) have length $3$.</p>

<p>There are $2274$ balanced integers below $10^4$.</p>

<p>Find the total number of balanced integers.  Give your answer modulo $10^9+7$.</p>
