Problem 71: Ordered Fractions
Consider the fraction n/d where n and d are positive integers. If n < d and HCF(n,d)=1, it is a reduced proper fraction. By listing reduced proper fractions for d ≤ 8: 1/8,1/7,1/6,1/5,1/4,2/7,1/3,3/8,2/5,3/7,1/2,4/7,3/5,5/8,2/3,5/7,3/4,4/5,5/6,6/7,7/8. 2/5 is the fraction immediately to the left of 3/7. By listing reduced proper fractions for d ≤ 1,000,000, find the numerator of the fraction immediately to the left of 3/7.
