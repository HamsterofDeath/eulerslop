Problem 91: Right Triangles with Integer Coordinates
The points P(x1,y1) and Q(x2,y2) are plotted at integer coordinates and are joined to the origin O(0,0), to form triangle OPQ. There are exactly fourteen triangles containing a right angle when coordinates are between 0 and 2 inclusive. Given that 0 ≤ x1, y1, x2, y2 ≤ 50, how many right triangles can be formed?
