Problem 43: Sub-string Divisibility

The number 1406357289 is a 0 to 9 pandigital number with an interesting sub-string divisibility property. Let d_i be the digits: d2d3d4=406 is divisible by 2, d3d4d5=063 by 3, d4d5d6=635 by 5, d5d6d7=357 by 7, d6d7d8=572 by 11, d7d8d9=728 by 13, d8d9d10=289 by 17. Find the sum of all 0 to 9 pandigital numbers with this property.
