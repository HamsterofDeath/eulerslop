Problem 96: Su Doku
Su Doku (Japanese meaning number place) is a popular puzzle. Replace blanks (or zeros) in a 9x9 grid such that each row, column, and 3x3 box contains digits 1 to 9. The 6K text file sudoku.txt contains fifty different Su Doku puzzles all with unique solutions. By solving all fifty puzzles find the sum of the 3-digit numbers found in the top left corner of each solution grid.
