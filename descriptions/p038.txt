Problem 38: Pandigital Multiples

Take the number 192 and multiply it by each of 1, 2, and 3: 192, 384, 576. Concatenating gives the 1 to 9 pandigital 192384576. The same can be achieved starting with 9 and multiplying by 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, giving the pandigital 918273645. What is the largest 1 to 9 pandigital 9-digit number that can be formed as the concatenated product of an integer with (1,2,...,n) where n > 1?
