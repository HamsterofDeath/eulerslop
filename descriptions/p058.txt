Problem 58: Spiral Primes
Starting with 1 and spiralling anticlockwise, a square spiral with side length 7 is formed where 8 out of 13 numbers on the diagonals are prime (ratio ~62%). If one complete new layer is wrapped around, a square spiral with side length 9 will be formed. If this process is continued, what is the side length of the square spiral for which the ratio of primes along both diagonals first falls below 10%?
