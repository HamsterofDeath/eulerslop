Problem 46: Goldbach's Other Conjecture

Christian Goldbach proposed every odd composite number can be written as the sum of a prime and twice a square: 9=7+2×1², 15=7+2×2², 21=3+2×3², 25=7+2×3², 27=19+2×2², 33=31+2×1². The conjecture was false. Find the smallest odd composite that cannot be written as sum of a prime and twice a square.
