Problem 100: Arranged Probability
If a box contains twenty-one coloured discs (fifteen blue, six red), taking two at random, P(BB) = (15/21)×(14/20) = 1/2. The next arrangement with exactly 50% chance of taking two blue discs is a box containing eighty-five blue and thirty-five red discs. By finding the first arrangement to contain over 10^12 = 1,000,000,000,000 discs in total, determine the number of blue discs that the box would contain.
